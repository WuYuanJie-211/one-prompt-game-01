Build a tiny browser game in a single self-contained index.html file (no external assets, no libraries): "Star Dodge", a neon arcade dodger. A little rocket ship at the bottom of a 1280x720 canvas moves with the arrow keys or the mouse. Meteors fall from the top at increasing speed; golden stars drift down too. Catch a star: +10 points and a chime. Hit a meteor: lose one of 3 lives, screen shake and a particle explosion. Speed and spawn rate ramp up as the score grows. Dark space background with twinkling stars and a nebula glow, neon colors, a HUD with score, lives and level, a start screen, and a game-over screen with the final score and restart on R or click. Add WebAudio synth sound effects (collect blip, hit thud, game-over jingle). All drawing on canvas, all code in the one file.
